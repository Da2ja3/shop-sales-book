# Shop Sales Book

A sales, stock and profit book for small shop owners. One page, runs fully in the browser, no account, no upload. Your data stays on your device.

## What it does

- **Record a sale** — pick a product, enter the quantity, and the sale is recorded. Stock drops automatically and profit is worked out from the cost price.
- **Today at a glance** — today's sales, today's profit, items sold, and products in stock, with your profit margin.
- **Products** — add products with a cost price, selling price and stock quantity. Products with 5 or fewer left are flagged LOW.
- **Undo a sale** — if you record a sale by mistake, undo it and the stock goes back.
- **Past days** — a day-by-day table of sales, revenue and profit.
- **Copy today's report** — plain text, ready to paste into WhatsApp.
- **Download my data** — a JSON backup of everything, so nothing is lost.

## How profit is worked out

Profit on each sale is (selling price − cost price) × quantity. If you sell at a different price on the day, enter that price in the sale form and the profit uses it.

## How to use it

1. Set your shop name (top right).
2. Add your products with their cost price, selling price and stock.
3. Record each sale as it happens.
4. At the end of the day, copy the report and share it, or check the past-days table.

## Notes

- Data is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use. It does not sync between devices.
- Works offline once the page is loaded.
